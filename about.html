About us

A one-person software studio in Duliajan, Assam.

Axomyq is the trading name of Axomyq LLP. The name joins Axom — Assam in Assamese — with yq, said like IQ.

The studio

Small, local, and accountable.

What we build

Two products of our own — BillBook for quotations and GST invoices, ContractZen for labour compliance and billing — plus websites, browser-based tools and AI-made content built to order.

Who it is for

Small firms, works contractors and the chartered accountants who look after them — anywhere in India. We began with the oil, gas and industrial belt of Upper Assam, and the same paperwork is done the same way from Guwahati to Gujarat.

How it is run

Lean, by design. The people who scope your work are the people who build it, so nothing is lost being handed along — and you get answers from someone who knows the code, not a ticket queue.

Where your data lives

Application data sits in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted in Mumbai, India, with row-level rules so one firm can never read another firm's records. Details in the privacy policy.
