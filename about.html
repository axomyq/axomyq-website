About us

A one-person software studio in Duliajan, Assam.

Axomyq is the trading name of Axomyq LLP. The name joins Axom — Assam in Assamese — with yq, said like IQ. Intelligence from the East is the promise we try to keep.

What we build

Two products of our own — BillBook for quotations and GST invoices, and ContractZen for labour compliance and billing — plus websites, browser-based tools and AI-made content for clients in the region.

Who it is for

Small firms, labour and works contractors serving the oil, gas and industrial belt of Upper Assam, and the chartered accountants who look after them. Most of our users are in the Duliajan–Tinsukia–Dibrugarh corridor.

How it is run

By its founder, Chandan Baruah, who designs, builds and supports everything himself. That keeps costs low and means the person you talk to is the person who fixes things.

Where your data lives

Application data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted in Mumbai, India, with row-level access rules so one firm can never read another firm's records. Details are in the privacy policy.
