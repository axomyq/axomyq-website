Axomyq BillBook

Quotation & GST invoice maker

Write a quotation, turn it into a GST invoice in one click, and keep payments and customer records together. Every document carries your firm details, GSTIN and bank details automatically.

Free until 30 September 2026. Message us and we set up your account at no cost.
What it does
  • Quotation to GST invoice in one click; proforma too
  • Multiple firms with separate numbering
  • Payment tracking with TDS and GST-TDS on receipts
  • GSTR-1 view, HSN summary, e-way bill JSON
  • Stock entries with automatic deduction on sale
  • Owner, Manager, Billing Clerk and Read-only roles
