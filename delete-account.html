Delete your account

For Axomyq BillBook and ContractZen. Last updated 7 September 2026.

Store requirement. Google Play requires this page to be linked from the Data safety form, and Apple requires an in-app deletion path as well (Guideline 5.1.1(v)). Section "From inside the app" must describe a real, working screen before submission — confirm it exists or build it.

From inside the app

  1. Sign in and open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account. [confirm exact menu location]
  3. Confirm with your password (and one-time code in ContractZen).

Only a firm Owner can delete a firm. Other users can delete their own login the same way.

Without the app (if you no longer have access)

  1. Email admin@axomyq.in from the email address on the account, with the subject Account deletion request.
  2. Include the app name (BillBook or ContractZen) and your firm name.
  3. We reply to that address to confirm it is you, then delete within [confirm: 30] days and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your login, name, email, mobile number and password hash
  • Firm profile, logo, bank details and settings
  • Customers, quotations, receipts, stock, attendance, payroll and register data
  • Uploaded files and attachments

What is kept, and for how long

  • Issued invoices and related tax records — retained, locked, for the period Indian tax law requires (currently up to 72 months from the due date of the relevant annual return) [confirm with your CA], then deleted.
  • Security logs — [confirm: 90] days.
  • Backups — deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to [confirm: 30] days before those backups expire.

Before you delete

Export anything you need first — invoices and registers export to PDF, Excel and CSV from inside the app. Deletion cannot be undone, and deactivating or "freezing" an account is not offered as an alternative.
