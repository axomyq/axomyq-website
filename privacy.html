Privacy policy

Applies to axomyq.in, app.axomyq.in (BillBook) and contractzen.axomyq.in (ContractZen). Last updated 7 September 2026.

Draft for your review. Everything below is written from how the apps are actually built. Items marked [confirm] need a yes/no or a figure from you before this goes live. Nothing here is a legal opinion — have a CA or advocate read it once.

Who we are

Axomyq LLP ("Axomyq", "we"), Duliajan, Assam, India, operates the website and the two applications. For anything in this policy, write to admin@axomyq.in.

What we collect

On the website (axomyq.in)

Nothing you type. The website has no forms, no analytics and no advertising cookies. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser, time) for security and to keep the site running. [confirm: no analytics tool is installed]

In BillBook and ContractZen

  • Account details — your name, email address, mobile number and password (stored only as a hash).
  • Firm details — firm name, address, GSTIN, PAN, bank details and logo that you enter for your documents.
  • Business records you create — customers, quotations, invoices, receipts, stock, attendance, payroll and statutory registers.
  • Employee records (ContractZen) — names, photographs, identity document numbers and bank details of workers that you, as their employer or contractor, enter. You are responsible for having the right to record this information.
  • Files you upload — attachments and compliance documents.
  • Usage and security logs — login times, one-time codes, and an audit trail of changes to records.

Why we use it

To run the service you signed up for: producing your documents, calculating tax and payroll, sending emails you request, keeping your account secure, and responding when you contact us. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

AI features

Some features — currently the AI quotation writer in BillBook [confirm the list] — send the text you type into that feature to Anthropic PBC, our AI provider, to generate a draft. Only that text is sent, only when you use the feature, and drafts are shown to you for review before anything is saved. Anthropic's handling of API data is described in their published terms. If you do not want to use AI features, simply do not use them; nothing else in the app depends on them.

How long we keep it

  • Account and business records — for as long as your account exists.
  • After you delete your account — most data is removed within [confirm: 30] days. Invoices and related tax records may be retained for the period Indian tax law requires (currently 72 months after the due date of the relevant annual return under the GST Act) [confirm with your CA], during which they are locked and not used for anything else.
  • Uploaded identity documents processed by our scan feature [confirm if live] — the source image is deleted after extraction; only the fields needed for the record are kept.
  • Server and security logs — [confirm: 90] days.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Firm data is separated at the database level with row-level security, so one firm cannot read another's records. ContractZen requires a one-time code as well as a password to sign in. Access to production systems is limited to the founder. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you without undue delay.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data inside the app at any time. You can ask us to export it, or delete your account by following these steps. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may also raise a grievance with us at the email above; we aim to resolve it within [confirm: 30] days. Children

The services are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will email account holders and update the date at the top. Continued use after that date means you accept the change.
