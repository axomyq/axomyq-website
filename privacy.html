Privacy policy

Applies to axomyq.in, app.axomyq.in (BillBook) and contractzen.axomyq.in (ContractZen). Last updated 7 September 2026.

Draft for your review. Written from how the apps are actually built. Items marked [confirm] need a figure or a yes/no from you before this goes live. Have a CA or advocate read it once.

Who we are

Axomyq LLP ("Axomyq", "we"), Duliajan, Assam, India, operates the website and the two applications. For anything in this policy, write to admin@axomyq.in.

What we collect

On the website

Nothing you type. The website has no forms, no analytics and no advertising cookies. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser, time) for security. [confirm: no analytics tool is installed]

In BillBook and ContractZen

  • Account details — name, email, mobile number and password (stored only as a hash).
  • Firm details — firm name, address, GSTIN, PAN, bank details and logo you enter for your documents.
  • Business records you create — customers, quotations, invoices, receipts, stock, attendance, payroll and registers.
  • Employee records (ContractZen) — names, photographs, identity document numbers and bank details of workers you enter. You are responsible for having the right to record this.
  • Files you upload — attachments and compliance documents.
  • Usage and security logs — login times, one-time codes, and an audit trail of changes.

Why we use it

To run the service you signed up for: producing your documents, calculating tax and payroll, sending emails you request, keeping your account secure, and answering you when you write. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

AI features

Some features — currently the AI quotation writer in BillBook [confirm the list] — send the text you type into that feature to Anthropic PBC to generate a draft. Only that text is sent, only when you use the feature, and drafts are shown to you before anything is saved. If you would rather not use AI features, simply do not use them; nothing else depends on them.

How long we keep it

  • Account and business records — while your account exists.
  • After deletion — most data removed within [confirm: 30] days. Invoices and related tax records may be retained for the period Indian tax law requires (currently 72 months after the due date of the relevant annual return) [confirm with your CA], locked and unused for anything else.
  • Server and security logs — [confirm: 90] days.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Firm data is separated at the database level with row-level security. ContractZen requires a one-time code as well as a password. Access to production systems is limited to the founder. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you without undue delay.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app at any time, ask us to export it, or delete your account by following these steps. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may raise a grievance with us at the email above; we aim to resolve it within [confirm: 30] days.

Children

The services are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

Material changes will be emailed to account holders and the date above updated.
